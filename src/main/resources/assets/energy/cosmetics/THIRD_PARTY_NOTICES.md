# Third-party cosmetic models

The following bundled models and textures are from the MIT-licensed
[HatCosmetics example resource pack](https://github.com/Supermarcel10/HatCosmetics/tree/master/assets/v2/ExampleResourcepack).
They were retrieved from the project's public `master` branch on 2026-09-05.
The downloaded JSON and PNG files are preserved without modification; Energy
loads the model geometry and textures through its own cosmetic renderer.

| Bundled files | Original model author | Original files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hatcosmetics/staff_hat.json`, `hatcosmetics/staff_hat.png` | Tonus_ (model credit: “Made by Tonus_ with Blockbench”) | [Model](https://github.com/Supermarcel10/HatCosmetics/blob/master/assets/v2/ExampleResourcepack/assets/minecraft/models/hatcosmetics/staff_hat.json), [texture](https://github.com/Supermarcel10/HatCosmetics/blob/master/assets/v2/ExampleResourcepack/assets/minecraft/textures/hatcosmetics/staff_hat.png) |
| `hatcosmetics/disguise.json`, `hatcosmetics/disguise.png` | red.cabbage (model credit: “Made by red.cabbage with Blockbench”) | [Model](https://github.com/Supermarcel10/HatCosmetics/blob/master/assets/v2/ExampleResourcepack/assets/minecraft/models/hatcosmetics/disguise.json), [texture](https://github.com/Supermarcel10/HatCosmetics/blob/master/assets/v2/ExampleResourcepack/assets/minecraft/textures/hatcosmetics/disguise.png) |
